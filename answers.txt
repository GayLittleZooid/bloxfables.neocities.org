// BITTER_NOSTALGIA
The hint is found on pages 1-3. It can easily be found by pressing ctrl-a or highlighting the empty space above the pages.
Using a cipher, shift the letters of "CHARRED PIZZA" back by two to get "AFYPPCB NGXXY". Afterwards, head to the Keyword Search on the Home page (aka the Prologue) and type it in. This will direct you to the page.


// VOID GROUP

// RACS 
Clicking on the ad on the ARTISTS page will redirect you to the Roblox Forums Archive. Press CTRL-A to see the hint at the bottom of the page easier. Going through the threads, there will be numbers shown in the account's total posts. 18 = R, 1 = A, 3 = C, 19 = S. Typing in "racs" at the end of the index/home page in the url will redirect you to a download for RACS. Enjoy shooting Rust some questions!
