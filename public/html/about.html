ABOUT

Welcome to the BLOX FABLES website! Blox Fables is an AU / story for ROBLOX, inspired by the history of the ROBLOX site and the community around it. Fun Fact: This entire story was originally going to be an RPG-- specifically a Paper Mario styled one. Unfortunately, I do not have the skillset for such a large-scope project by myself, so here's the comprimise! I hope you enjoy the stories that Tato and I have to tell.

"What kind of story is this?"

A classic "unlikely trio bands together for a quest" type story. Best way I could describe it is one of those common RPG / Fantasy stories with a ROBLOXian twist! The story follows a Noob, a Guest, and an NPC being forced together for a competition, and sticking together as the world seemingly unravels. Or at least that's the BLOX FABLES plot line!

Tato's in charge of the other story-- Charred Pizza- while focus specifically on Elliot, Dued1, and 007n7's relationship before the events of the main story.

"Multimedia?" Yes this is multimedia. I promise. Not just in the sense of "written like a light novel" or one story and illustrated comics for the other. I guess the best way to describe this would be an ARG? Scattered across this site are many secrets, which expand upon the lore the POVs of the characters can't see, and offer early answers and much more for y'all!

"Secrets? How can I find them?"

I have lots of secrets on this site, and am only planning to add more in the future! While I don't want to go into detail about how to find them so people can find them on their own, if you are struggling I highly reccomend using your browser's inspect tool or going onto this site's GITHUB repository! There, you can comb through the elements/files yourself and find the code to make this bullshit possible.

On the site's github, there's a file dedicated to those wanting answers. They will have the locations of all the secrets and how to find them! If you want some hints... best I can offer is reloading certain pages, ctrl-a, and clicking whatever you can. Happy hunting!

Personally, I don't mind people decompiling my work. As long as it's not to copy-paste my code and claim as your own. I think if somebody goes through the effort of decompiling or combing through my trash code to find the answers, then they've earned it.

"How can I support?"

If you really like this project and want to support and see more of it, there's plenty of options! You can send asks to the BUGGALOG Tumblr, or here in our very own GUESTBOOK! It absolutely makes my day to see comments, fan-art, and everything else y'all cook up.

This is just a side project, unfortunately. I personally want to keep my works free for everyone to see, especially in the current state of the world where money is tight. I don't monetize my work, nor offer any extra benefits for people to do, so everybody gets the full experience. Also this is based on ROBLOX I can't monetize that LOL.

If you really do want to show financial support for what we do, you can commission me or Tato or donate to our Ko-Fi's. Again, this will have no benefits for the donator.
